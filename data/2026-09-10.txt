Why does this matter? Autonomous artificial intelligence models undergoing red-team evaluations for offensive cyber capabilities require total network containment. ### 3. Beijing Rejects U.S. Intelligence Distillation Advisory as Protectionist "AI Monopoly" Campaign

#### [Overview]
Why does this matter? Frontier foundation model pre-training requires hundreds of millions of dollars in compute, advanced silicon, and multi-megawatt facilities. While widely accepted in academic research, systematic commercial extraction of proprietary reasoning outputs from foreign cloud APIs sits in a regulatory grey zone. When national security agencies formally classify high-volume API distillation as state-sponsored intellectual property exfiltration, inference endpoints become an active battleground for trade enforcement and export controls.

On September 9, 2026, the Chinese Government issued a diplomatic rebuttal rejecting claims in a joint Cybersecurity Advisory released hours earlier by the U.S. National Security Agency (NSA), Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), and Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). The advisory claimed Chinese entities bypassed terms of service by rotating commercial cloud proxies and token aggregators to extract billions of output tokens. On September 9, Beijing characterized the advisory as an effort to enforce an "American AI monopoly," maintaining that distillation and synthetic data training are universal scientific methods, and warned of reciprocal trade countermeasures.

### 4. Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek Unveil 2.1D Interposer-Less Substrates, 100mm+ FC-BGA, and Glass Cores at KPCA Show 2026 (Category 6)

**Source:** Samsung Electro-Mechanics / LG Innotek / Seoul Economic Daily / Maeil Business Newspaper (MK)  
**Event Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Source Date:** 2026-09-09, 17:23 KST  
**Status:** Commercial Semiconductor Substrate Unveiling & Packaging Roadmap Presentation  
**Primary URL:** https://www.sedaily.com/NewsView/20260909SED

#### [Overview]
Why does this matter? As AI accelerators expand in silicon footprint—integrating multiple large compute dies alongside multiple High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) stacks—the package substrate has become a primary bottleneck for system-level electrical and thermomechanical performance. In standard 2.5D packaging (such as TSMC CoWoS-S), chiplets are placed onto an intermediate silicon interposer, which is then mounted onto a multi-layer organic flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrate. However, silicon interposers add manufacturing complexity, increase vertical package profile height, and require multi-reticle stitching. Concurrently, as package substrates scale beyond 85mm per side to accommodate larger chiplet configurations, conventional organic core materials experience thermomechanical warpage during solder reflow, threatening micro-bump integrity and signal connections across high-speed SerDes and power pins.

On September 9, 2026, at KPCA Show 2026 (held at Songdo Convensia in Incheon), South Korean substrate leaders Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek unveiled next-generation AI packaging technologies. **Samsung Electro-Mechanics introduced its 2.1D package substrate technology**, which connects adjacent logic and memory chiplets using fine-line redistribution layers (RDL) formed directly within the substrate, eliminating the need for an intermediate silicon interposer. By removing the silicon interposer, the 2.1D architecture lowers package thickness and simplifies the packaging stack, altering the design trade-off between inter-chip routing density and production cost. Alongside 2.1D, Samsung demonstrated server-class 2.5D FC-BGA substrates engineered with warpage-suppression layers and displayed Glass Core Substrates (GCS) featuring fine-pitch Through-Glass Vias (TGVs) filled with metal. Simultaneously, **LG Innotek publicly displayed an ultra-large FC-BGA substrate designed for AI accelerators measuring over 100mm by 100mm**—representing an area expansion of approximately 38.4% over standard 85mm x 85mm platforms. LG Innotek also showcased in-substrate chip-embedding techniques that integrate thin passive decoupling components directly into the inner substrate core layers, reducing loop inductance to stabilize high-transient power delivery.

System-level electrical, thermal, and mechanical co-design implications:
1. **2.1D Interposer-Less Routing Architecture:** Standard 2.5D packages use an intermediate silicon interposer with sub-micron line/space lithography. Samsung's 2.1D platform constructs high-density organic RDL directly on the substrate core. Eliminating the silicon interposer removes a bonding interface, decreasing overall package z-height and reducing assembly stack thermal resistance, while shifting signal routing into low-loss organic dielectric materials.
2. **PDN Loop Inductance & Substrate Component Embedding:** Frontier AI accelerators operate at core supply voltages below 0.8V while drawing large dynamic transient currents. LG Innotek’s chip-embedding technology places thin capacitive components inside the substrate core rather than exclusively on the outer board surface. Shortening the vertical physical distance from decoupling capacitors to the accelerator logic dies slashes power-delivery network (PDN) loop inductance, damping high-frequency voltage droop during workload burst cycles.
3. **Glass Core Transition & 100mm+ Dimensional Warpage:** Scaling FC-BGA substrates beyond 100mm x 100mm increases warpage vulnerability during thermal reflow due to the coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) mismatch between silicon dies and organic resin substrates. Glass core substrates provide high mechanical stiffness and a CTE closely matched to silicon, reducing reflow warpage on large-area modules and maintaining coplanarity across tens of thousands of fine-pitch solder balls.

#### [Strategic Impact]

- **Technology & Ecosystem:** Broadens heterogeneous integration packaging options, offering fabless chip designers alternatives to traditional silicon-interposer-based 2.5D assemblies through interposer-less 2.1D substrates and embedded packaging.
- **Business & Market:** Enables Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek to challenge the market share of major packaging and substrate incumbents—such as Ibiden (Japan) and Unimicron (Taiwan)—in the high-margin market for AI server substrates.
- **Infrastructure & Hardware:** Provides physical packaging support for accelerator packages exceeding 100mm x 100mm, accommodating expanded compute logic and HBM arrays on a unified substrate interface.
- **Investment Implication:** Overweight advanced substrate material suppliers, laser TGV drilling tool manufacturers, and high-density substrate automated optical inspection (AOI) providers; monitor commercial qualification progress for glass substrates.

Key specifications:

#### [Critical View]
Although eliminating the silicon interposer in 2.1D substrates reduces assembly complexity, organic fine-line RDL generally provides lower routing density than silicon-based interposers, requiring wider spacing between chiplet interfaces. Scaling monolithic organic substrates beyond 100mm x 100mm poses severe yield challenges: an electrical short or defect in any of the internal build-up layers ruins the entire multi-layer panel. While glass core substrates mitigate thermomechanical warpage, industry-wide challenges remain in automated high-speed handling of brittle glass panels, crack propagation during TGV drilling, and thermal stress reliability under cycling, keeping high-volume mass production targeted for 2028.
