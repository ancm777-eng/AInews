    Key Hardware Architecture Concepts

    Offset PoP (Package-on-Package): An advanced heterogeneous packaging geometry where top-layer DRAM is laterally displaced from the silicon center to expose high-power compute clusters directly to external thermal interface materials.

    Junction-to-Case Thermal Resistance: The conductive thermal impedance between the active transistor junction and the chip exterior; decoupled in offset designs to prevent memory cell leakage at elevated compute temperatures.

    RDL Trace Skew Matching: Redistribution layer routing techniques that equalize asymmetric physical propagation lengths to maintain signal integrity at memory transfer rates up to 5,300 MHz.
