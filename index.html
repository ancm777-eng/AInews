Marvell Showcases Switchless Optical Photonic Fabric (32 TB Pool) and Six-Channel Structera X CXL 3.2 Expanders at SC26

1. Executive Summary

As long-context reasoning and multi-step agents expand Key-Value (KV) caches past on-package High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), copper links and multi-hop switches introduce severe attenuation and queuing stalls. Ahead of Supercomputing 2026 (SC26, Nov 15–20), Marvell unveiled a switchless optical Photonic Fabric Memory Module alongside second-generation Structera X 3406/3506 PCIe Gen 6 CXL 3.2 controllers.

Using direct optical I/O and a passive fiber shuffle, Photonic Fabric pools up to 32 TB of warm KV cache across 16 host nodes at ~350 ns pod access latency, while Structera X unlocks up to 9 TB (DDR4) or 6 TB (DDR5) with 250 GB/s hardware LZ4 compression—providing merchant-silicon alternatives to proprietary NVLink domains.

Key Hardware & Packaging Concepts

  • CXL 3.2 (Compute Express Link): A high-speed coherent memory-expansion standard over PCIe Gen 6 enabling multi-host memory pooling and cache-coherent sharing across server CPUs and accelerators.
  • PAM4 & 224G SerDes: 4-level pulse-amplitude modulation signaling (64 GT/s per PCIe Gen 6 lane) that doubles bandwidth per cycle but limits passive copper reach to ~1–2 meters without active retimers or AECs.
  • Switchless Passive Optical Shuffle: An all-optical crossbar routing direct optical I/O between 16 hosts and shared memory without power-hungry electronic switch ASICs, eliminating switch thermal density.

2. Architecture & Performance

At PCIe Gen 6 (64 GT/s PAM4) and 224G SerDes rates, copper backplane attenuation forces expensive laminates and retimers. Replacing switch ASICs with a passive optical fiber shuffle extends reach up to ~50 meters (~25–30 meters practical design target given ~5 ns/m one-way fiber delay), while Photonic Fabric chiplets (from Celestial AI) target 2–3x inference throughput gains.

Complementing optical pooling, Structera X 3406/3506 scales from 4 to 6 memory channels with 4-host CXL 3.2 sharing, 180–300 ns target latency, 5x faster LZ4 compression (250 GB/s), and AES-256 / Caliptra 2.0 security—paired at SC26 with 102.4 Tbps Teralynx T100 switches, Alaska A 1.6T/800G AECs, and Bravera SC6 SSD controllers.
