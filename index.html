    Packaging Disruption: Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek Advance 2.1D and 100mm+ FC-BGA

    1. Abstract

    At KPCA Show 2026 in Songdo, Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek revealed next-generation packaging architectures designed to resolve thermomechanical and power delivery limits in AI accelerators. Samsung showcased 2.1D interposer-less substrates connecting logic and High Bandwidth Memory directly via organic redistribution layers alongside glass core prototypes. Concurrently, LG Innotek unveiled an ultra-large FC-BGA substrate exceeding 100mm × 100mm with embedded passive decoupling capacitors to minimize high-frequency transient impedance.

    Key Packaging Concepts:

    • 2.1D Substrate: An organic substrate architecture that routes die-to-die chiplet interconnects directly within high-density surface redistribution layers, bypassing discrete silicon interposers.
    • PDN Loop Inductance: Parasitic impedance along power distribution networks that induces severe voltage droop under ultra-fast sub-0.8V dynamic current steps.
    • Glass Core Substrate (GCS): A rigid packaging core utilizing Through-Glass Vias (TGVs) to match silicon's thermal expansion coefficient and prevent large-panel reflow warpage.
    • In-Substrate Component Embedding: Burying thin passive capacitors directly inside core layers to eliminate vertical parasitic distance to compute dies.

    2. Architecture & Performance

    As accelerator compute footprints scale to host multiple compute dies and dense HBM stacks, standard 2.5D packaging faces critical thermal and manufacturing bottlenecks. Intermediate silicon interposers incur reticle-stitching limits, vertical z-height penalties, and elevated assembly costs. Simultaneously, expanding monolithic organic substrates beyond 85mm introduces thermomechanical warpage during reflow, while lower supply voltages (<0.8V) require dramatic reductions in power delivery network (PDN) loop inductance.
