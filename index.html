    1. Executive Summary

By replacing monolithic trajectory sampling with a compact rank-384 LoRA adapter and a distilled 24-layer noncausal VAE decoder, creators can synthesize low-resolution drafts and upgrade them to broadcast-ready 2560×1440 (2K) footage in just three sampling steps.     2. Architecture & Performance

    The core innovation of the Open-H3-Regenerate-2K framework lies in decoupling coarse frame dynamics from high-frequency spatial texture synthesis. Traditional text-to-video diffusion models recalculate global self-attention across 30 to 50 iterations at full pixel dimensions, causing quadratic memory pressure on client VRAM. Instead, this multi-stage pipeline synthesizes motion at 480p or 768p and applies a 3-step diffusion pass via a ~6 GB BF16 adapter to regenerate fine textures, micro-surfaces, and sharp contours.
