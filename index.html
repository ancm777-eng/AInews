    Integrated Voltage Regulator (IVR): Miniaturized power management circuitry co-packaged directly adjacent to logic silicon on advanced interposers, stepping down higher intermediate voltages to suppress board-level resistive losses and minimize loop inductance.
    TSMC-COUPE: Compact Universal Photonic Engine integrating Electronic ICs (EIC) directly with Photonic ICs (PIC) via 3D hybrid bonding to provide low-energy optical interconnect lanes.

Supplying an intermediate bus voltage to an on-package IVR reduces physical separation to millimeters, slashing parasitic inductance and providing nanosecond transient regulation for sub-0.75V, kiloampere-class loads.
