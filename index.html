    SENKO, Advantest, and VIAVI Unveil Automated Module-Level Testing Architecture for Co-Packaged Optics at ECOC 2026

    1. Abstract

    On September 14, 2026, optical interconnect developer SENKO Advanced Components, automated test equipment (ATE) supplier Advantest Corporation, and photonic instrumentation provider VIAVI Solutions jointly announced an automated module-level testing platform for Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) architectures ahead of ECOC 2026 in Málaga, Spain. The collaborative platform resolves the primary high-volume manufacturing (HVM) bottleneck at the Insertion 4 module-level test stage, utilizing detachable Very Small Form Factor (VSFF) optical interfaces to execute automated, repeatable multi-channel electro-optic parametric and Bit Error Rate (BER) sweeps for 200 Gbps per lane PAM4 (1.6 Tbps aggregate) optical engines.

    Co-Packaged Optics (CPO): An advanced packaging architecture that mounts optical engines directly onto the common package substrate alongside compute accelerators or switch ASICs, reducing electrical channel trace lengths to millimeters.

    Insertion 4 Testing: The critical post-packaging manufacturing test phase where the fully integrated multi-die module (ASIC plus adjacent optical engines) undergoes full-speed functional and electro-optic validation.

    VSFF Connector (SN-MT): Very Small Form Factor multi-fiber detachable optical connectors that replace permanently spliced fiber pigtails, allowing automated robotic pick-and-place blind mating within ATE sockets.

    TDECQ: Transmitter Dispersion Eye Closure Quaternary; a fundamental metric quantifying optical transmitter eye degradation and noise penalties in high-speed PAM4 signaling.

    2. Architecture & Performance

    Scaling datacenter switching backplanes toward 1.6T and 3.2T boundaries causes traditional pluggable transceivers to encounter severe front-panel thermal dissipation limits and unrecoverable high-frequency electrical channel losses across host printed circuit boards. While CPO shortens electrical traces, commercialization has been obstructed by the lack of automated test methodologies: legacy optical engines utilized permanently spliced fiber pigtails, which are physically incompatible with high-throughput ATE handlers.

    3. Strategic Outlook & Limitations

    While detachable VSFF optical connectors bypass the fragility of permanent pigtails, automated optical testing introduces acute environmental cleanroom requirements. At 200 Gbps per lane, sub-micron particle contamination or microscopic dust settling on connector end-faces causes catastrophic insertion loss and return-loss degradation. Integrating high-throughput robotic handlers into outsourced assembly and test (OSAT) environments will necessitate automated in-situ ferrule inspection and dynamic air-cleaning mechanisms to sustain required manufacturing yields.

    TSMC Outlines 2027 Advanced Node Roadmap and Targets Doubling CoWoS Packaging Capacity to 260,000 Wafers by 2028

    1. Abstract

    On September 14, 2026, Taiwanese semiconductor supply-chain disclosures confirmed that TSMC outlined its 2027 advanced node expansion roadmap, projecting a 22% increase in 2nm capacity (to 110,000 wafers monthly) and a 16.7% increase in 3nm capacity (to 210,000 wafers monthly) by mid-2027. To eliminate the acute packaging bottleneck constraining frontier AI accelerator deployments, TSMC confirmed capital commitments to double its Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate (CoWoS) advanced packaging capacity from approximately 130,000 wafers per month at end-2026 to 260,000 wafers per month by the end of 2028, deploying dual-region packaging lines across its Chiayi AP7 facility in Taiwan and its Arizona advanced packaging campus.

    Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate (CoWoS): TSMC's 2.5D advanced packaging technology that mounts compute logic dies alongside High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) on a high-density silicon or RDL interposer.

    Interposer Reticle Stitching: Lithographic exposure techniques combining multiple reticle fields (up to 3.3x–5.5x reticle size) to support massive multi-die compute fabrics exceeding 1,000 mm².

    Power Delivery Network (PDN): Low-impedance vertical power distribution channels incorporating Deep Trench Capacitors (DTC) to suppress transient voltage droop at sub-0.8V operating levels exceeding 1,000A.

    CTE Mismatch: The divergence in Coefficient of Thermal Expansion between silicon interposers (~2.6 ppm/°C) and organic substrates (~14 ppm/°C), inducing substrate warpage during high-temperature thermal reflow.

    2. Architecture & Performance

    Monolithic die scaling has hit fundamental lithographic limits (~858 mm² mask size), compelling AI accelerator architectures toward multi-die partitioning interconnected across wide-bus silicon interposers. CoWoS packaging serves as the indispensable physical foundation for linking accelerator logic with 8 to 12 stacks of HBM3E and HBM4. However, severe interposer capacity constraints have capped hyperscaler accelerator deployments worldwide.
