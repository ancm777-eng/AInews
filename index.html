    Microchip and Navitas Deliver 800V DC-to-6V DC Power Conversion Reference Architecture for OCP AI Server Racks

    1. Executive Summary

    As next-generation AI accelerator racks surpass 120 kW to 200 kW, conventional 48V/54V power distribution architectures incur crippling resistive $I^2R$ conduction losses that demand heavy, space-consuming copper busbars. Microchip Technology and Navitas Semiconductor resolved this power density crisis by introducing a direct 800V DC-to-6V DC power distribution reference architecture aligned with Open Compute Project (OCP) standards. Utilizing 16 series-stacked Navitas NV6034 650V GaNFast FETs operating at a 1.0 MHz switching frequency, the system eliminates traditional intermediate transformation steps to achieve a volumetric power density of 2,100 W/in³ and 96% peak efficiency. Regulated by Microchip's dsPIC33AK controller featuring 78-picosecond PWM timing resolution, this ultra-low-profile board routes high-voltage direct current adjacent to compute dies, shrinking board-level power footprint while maximizing server rack volumetric payload.

    Wide-Bandgap (WBG) GaN: Semiconductor material offering superior electron mobility and breakdown electric fields, allowing megahertz-level switching with minimal parasitic capacitive losses.

    Point-of-Load (PoL) Converter: Board-level DC-DC voltage conversion stage positioned directly adjacent to high-current compute silicon to mitigate trace resistance.

    Series-Stacked Half-Bridge: Circuit topology distributing high aggregate DC bus voltages across multiple lower-voltage-rated power switches to divide electrical stress evenly.

    Digital Signal Controller (DSC): Real-time processor combining MCU peripheral control with DSP mathematical execution for sub-nanosecond pulse-width modulation.

    2. Architecture & Performance

    The single-stage reference design completely displaces the legacy 48V intermediary shelf. High-voltage 800V DC bus rails feed directly into series-stacked GaNFast half-bridge arrays on the motherboard carrier, producing an intermediate 6V rail that multiphase regulators drop to sub-1V $V_{core}$ levels.
