    Industrial HiPIMS Sputtering Resolves Through-Glass Via Metallization for Next-Gen AI Glass Substrates

    1. Abstract

    At SEMICON Taiwan 2026, TRUMPF commercially unveiled its industrial High-Power Impulse Magnetron Sputtering (HiPIMS) deposition platform, overcoming the definitive manufacturing yield barrier in semiconductor glass core packaging substrates: Through-Glass Via (TGV) metallization voiding. By generating an ultra-dense, highly ionized metal plasma steered through directional RF substrate biasing, HiPIMS achieves continuous, uniform copper seed layer deposition inside micro-vias with aspect ratios exceeding 10:1 to 15:1. This breakthrough prevents sidewall pinch-off and latent open-circuit micro-voids, enabling the crack-free industrial production of ultra-large format glass substrates (>100mm x 100mm) capable of supporting 1,000W+ next-generation heterogeneous AI accelerators integrating 16-Hi HBM4 stacks.

    Key Architectural Concepts

    HiPIMS (High-Power Impulse Magnetron Sputtering): A pulsed plasma sputtering technique generating high-density ionized metal species that can be directionally steered into high-aspect-ratio micro-cavities.

    TGV (Through-Glass Via): Vertical conductive copper channels laser-drilled and metallized through thin glass panels to interconnect top-die micro-bumps with bottom package substrates.

    ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film): Conventional organic resin dielectric laminates increasingly vulnerable to high-temperature thermal warpage and high-frequency insertion losses.

    CTE Matching (Coefficient of Thermal Expansion): Matching the glass core's thermal expansion coefficient (3.2 ppm/K) closely to silicon (2.6 ppm/K) to eliminate thermomechanical stress and micro-bump shearing.

    2. Architecture & Performance

    Replacing traditional organic cores with glass substrates rectifies structural physical limitations in high-speed PAM4 SerDes transmission, power distribution loop inductance, and mechanical package planarity under sustained thermal loads.
