    Navitas and NVIDIA Demonstrate 24 kW 800V-to-12V Single-Stage GaN Power Delivery for MGX Racks

    1. Executive Summary

    Distributing power across 100 kW to 250 kW megawatt AI racks using legacy 48V/54V architectures causes severe I²R resistive thermal dissipation, thick copper busbars, and volumetric inefficiencies. To solve this physical distribution bottleneck, Navitas and NVIDIA engineered a 24 kW single-stage GaN Power Delivery Board (PDB) that steps 800 VDC down directly to 12 VDC within NVIDIA's standard 400 mm × 60 mm MGX compute tray envelope. Utilizing 16 primary-side NV6034 (650V) GaNFast FETs, this architecture delivers over 2,000 W/in³ volumetric power density at 97.0% peak conversion efficiency, cutting rack distribution losses by up to 256 times.

    Single-Stage Direct Conversion: A power topology that steps down high facility distribution voltages (800 VDC) directly to board-level voltages (12 VDC) in one step, bypassing legacy intermediate bus converter stages.

    Wide-Bandgap (WBG) Gallium Nitride: Advanced semiconductor material enabling high switching frequencies, superior breakdown voltage thresholds, and minimal switching losses compared to standard silicon MOSFETs.

    I²R Resistive Conduction Loss: Electrical dissipation where power loss scales with the square of current; increasing distribution voltage to 800V reduces current sixteenfold, slashing thermal busbar losses.
