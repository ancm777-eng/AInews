    Co-Packaged Optics (CPO): An advanced packaging paradigm integrating optical I/O dies directly on-substrate with compute ASICs, collapsing electrical high-frequency interconnect traces from inches to millimeters.
    Micro-Ring Modulator (MRM): Ultra-compact sub-micron silicon photonic resonators that modulate continuous-wave light wavelengths while minimizing capacitive load and thermal footprint.
    External CW Laser (SuperNova): A decoupled optical continuous-wave light source isolating thermally sensitive Indium Phosphide (InP) laser diodes from high-heat compute accelerators.
    UCIe Die-to-Die Interconnect: Standardized multi-die physical interface delivering multi-terabit bandwidth between the compute silicon and optical transceivers with sub-2 dB channel loss.

While conventional pluggable optical transceivers bridge physical rack spans, their power-hungry DSP retimers consume significant faceplate and thermal margins. Ayar Labs' TeraPHY architecture bypasses PCB losses by interfacing directly with host GPUs and switches via 2.5D substrate interposers or UCIe channels.
