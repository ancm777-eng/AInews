### 4. Qnity Unveils Integrated Materials Platform at SEMICON Taiwan for AI Chip Packaging, TGVs, and Glass Core Substrates (Category 6)

**Source:** Qnity Electronics Inc. (NYSE: Q) / SEMICON Taiwan 2026 / MyChesCo / Barchart  
**Event Date:** 2026-09-01 (Technical Presentation) / 2026-09-04 (Technical Platform Analysis)  
**Source Date:** 2026-09-04, 14:00 EDT / 2026-09-05, 03:00 KST  
**Status:** Commercial Semiconductor Materials Release & Advanced Packaging Roadmap Disclosures  
**Primary URL:** https://www.mychesco.com/a/news/business/qnity-targets-ai-chip-packaging-with-integrated-materials/  

#### [Overview]
Why does this matter? As AI accelerators expand to multi-chiplet modules integrating dozens of compute tiles and High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) stacks, conventional organic packaging substrates encounter fatal physical barriers. Substrates exceeding 100mm by 100mm suffer severe thermomechanical warpage during reflow, which cracks microscopic solder bumps and disrupts high-speed interconnects. While glass core substrates and advanced 2.5D/3D architectures solve warpage, they introduce severe chemical and material challenges: metallizing and filling high-aspect-ratio Through-Glass Vias (TGVs) without voids, maintaining sub-2-micrometer line/space patterning, and ensuring uniform micro-bump coplanarity across massive multi-die surfaces.

At SEMICON Taiwan 2026 in Taipei, Jason Chuang, Packaging Polymer Segment Director at Qnity Electronics (NYSE: Q), detailed the company’s new integrated semiconductor materials platform. Designed specifically to resolve integration bottlenecks across 2.5D, 3D, and Panel-Level Packaging (PLP), Qnity's platform unifies specialized dielectric formulations, photoresists, and electroplating chemistries. To address the glass packaging transition, Qnity introduced **Cyclotene**, an advanced photo-imageable dielectric film that fills Through-Glass Vias (TGVs) void-free, providing the electrical insulation and mechanical support required to route ultra-dense vertical connections without inducing glass micro-cracks. For high-density micro-bump formation, Qnity showcased **Riston WBR5000**, a specialized dry-film photoresist available in thicknesses from 200 to 320 micrometers, optimized for vertical copper-pillar plating with tight critical dimension (CD) control. These polymers are paired with Qnity's **Intervia CB1000+ and CB3000** copper electroplating chemistries, which achieve exceptional within-die (WID) uniformity and minimize total indicated runout (TIR), preventing height variations across thousands of micro-bumps that cause cold-joint soldering defects in 16-Hi HBM4 and multi-tile accelerator packages.

System-level electrical, thermal, and manufacturing co-design implications:
1. **Signal Integrity (SI) & High-Frequency SerDes:** Utilizing Cyclotene within glass-core substrates provides a low dielectric dissipation factor (Df < 0.003) compared to traditional epoxy-based Ajinomoto Build-up Films (ABF, Df ~0.015). This reduces channel insertion loss by more than 40% across 224G and 448G PAM4 SerDes traces, eliminating the need for intermediary retimer chips between the accelerator package and high-speed networking fabrics.
2. **Power Integrity (PI) & Transient Droop Suppression:** Conformal, void-free copper fill inside TGVs combined with Intervia plating chemistry slashes vertical interconnect parasitic resistance and via loop inductance. For 1,000W+ AI accelerators drawing transient currents exceeding 1,200 amperes, low-resistance vertical through-via channels suppress dynamic voltage droop during sudden computational bursts.
3. **Thermomechanical Warpage & Bump Coplanarity:** Glass substrates provide a tunable coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE 3.2 ppm/K) closely matched to silicon (2.6 ppm/K), preventing package bowing across thermal cycles. Qnity’s Riston WBR5000 delivers strict micro-bump height uniformity across 200–320 micrometer resist coats, ensuring that when multi-die arrays are bonded via thermo-compression or direct hybrid bonding, every micro-joint achieves planar, void-free contact.

#### [Strategic Impact]

- **Technology & Ecosystem:** Solves critical material-level defects in advanced packaging, allowing OSATs and foundries (TSMC CoWoS, Intel EMIB, ASE) to transition multi-chiplet AI packaging from organic substrates to glass and panel formats.
- **Business & Market:** Positions Qnity as an essential Tier-2 chemical materials supplier across the AI hardware supply chain, capturing high-margin recurring chemical sales as HBM4 and custom ASICs scale.
- **Infrastructure & Hardware:** Enables hardware architects to design larger AI accelerator packages (>100mm x 100mm) housing up to 16 HBM stacks without facing substrate warpage failures.
- **Investment Implication:** Overweight specialty semiconductor materials and wet chemical formulation leaders (Qnity, Resonac, Tokyo Ohka Kogyo); recognize that advanced packaging scaling depends as much on polymer chemistry as on lithography tools.
