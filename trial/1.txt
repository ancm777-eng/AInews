### 4. JCET Secures $968M Private Placement to Expand High-Density SiP and Wafer-Level Packaging for AI Accelerators (Category 6)

**Source:** JCET Group / Shanghai Stock Exchange Regulatory Disclosures / Digitimes / KuCoin News  
**Event Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Source Date:** 2026-09-07, 07:42 CST / 08:42 KST

**Primary URL:** https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260904PD203/jcet-ai-chip-packaging-china-capex.html  

#### [Overview]
Why does this matter? As AI accelerators scale beyond standard reticle dimensions (exceeding 858 square millimeters) to integrate multi-chiplet logic tiles alongside multiple High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) stacks, conventional single-die monolithic packaging is no longer physically viable. Modern accelerators require advanced heterogeneous packaging—specifically 2.5D interposers, high-density System-in-Package (SiP), and fan-out wafer-level packaging (FOWLP)—to route tens of thousands of high-speed parallel traces between logic and memory. However, global advanced packaging capacity is operating near 100% utilization, creating a severe manufacturing chokepoint that limits total AI accelerator shipments even when leading-edge wafer fab capacity is available.

On September 7, 2026, JCET Group (Jiangsu Changjiang Electronics Tech, SSE: 600584), the world’s third-largest and China’s largest semiconductor assembly and test provider, officially announced a non-public share placement raising up to **RMB 6.5 billion (approximately $968 million USD)**. This capital injection directly funds the construction of high-density advanced packaging lines engineered specifically for artificial intelligence processors, multi-chiplet modules, and high-bandwidth heterogeneous integration. Driven by surging contract demand from major fabless clients including Nvidia and AMD, JCET lifted its total 2026 capital expenditure target to **CNY 10 billion ($1.4 billion USD)**. The company disclosed that its existing advanced packaging and wafer-level testing lines are operating at peak utilization due to the severe bandwidth, thermomechanical warpage, and power delivery demands of next-generation AI accelerators. The new facilities will focus on high-density System-in-Package (SiP), fine-pitch redistribution layer (RDL) interposers, and advanced wafer-level packaging (WLP) capable of supporting multi-die accelerator assemblies operating at 1,000 watts or higher.

System-level electrical, thermal, and packaging co-design implications:
1. **Redistribution Layer (RDL) & Interconnect Pitch:** High-density SiP and wafer-level packaging utilize sub-2 micrometer line/space (L/S) RDL traces to interconnect host logic chiplets with adjacent HBM stacks. Compared to standard organic substrates (which operate at 10 to 15 micrometer pitches), fine-pitch RDL provides over five times the interconnect wire density, drastically shortening trace lengths and lowering signal transmission latency.
2. **Power Distribution Network (PDN) & IR Drop:** For AI accelerator modules drawing transient currents exceeding 1,200 amperes at sub-0.8V core logic levels, vertical parasitic resistance induces severe dynamic voltage droop. JCET’s wafer-level packaging integrates thick copper power redistribution planes and embedded deep-trench capacitors (DTC) directly within the base interposer, stabilizing the power grid against steep current steps.
3. **Thermomechanical Warpage & Thermal Interface Material (TIM):** Stacking multiple large silicon dies on organic or glass interposers introduces extreme thermal stress due to mismatched coefficients of thermal expansion (CTE). JCET’s advanced SiP lines incorporate specialized low-stress epoxy mold compounds (EMC) and precision bond-line thickness (BLT) control for metallic thermal interface materials, facilitating efficient vertical heat transfer from 1,000W dies into server cold plates without inducing micro-joint solder fracture.

#### [Strategic Impact]

- **Technology & Ecosystem:** Accelerates the commercial transition from monolithic silicon toward modular multi-chiplet System-in-Package architectures, providing tier-1 and domestic chip designers with expanded packaging alternatives to TSMC CoWoS.
- **Business & Market:** Solidifies JCET’s market share in the global outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) hierarchy, allowing it to capture high-margin advanced packaging revenue as AI hardware demand outstrips foundry packaging capacity.
- **Infrastructure & Hardware:** Expands the aggregate global supply of 2.5D/SiP packaging capacity, directly alleviating the physical manufacturing bottleneck that governs the delivery lead times of AI compute clusters.
- **Investment Implication:** Overweight advanced packaging capital equipment vendors (Besi, Disco, ASMPT), fine-pitch electroplating chemistry suppliers, and specialized OSAT market leaders; track packaging capacity utilization metrics as leading indicators of AI chip delivery.
