Why this architecture matters: Large-scale agentic coding and multi-turn reasoning require models to ingest long contexts (up to 1 million tokens) without encountering the severe Key-Value (KV) cache memory explosion associated with standard quadratic attention mechanisms. Trained on a fresh 30-trillion-token multimodal dataset, GLM-5.3-Flash integrates a total of 320 billion parameters while dynamically routing only 18 billion active parameters per token across 288 sparse experts (activating 8 routed experts plus 1 shared expert per layer). This design delivers an estimated 3x reduction in attention compute and a 4x reduction in KV-cache memory pressure relative to full-attention architectures, allowing the native FP8 checkpoint to be served efficiently across multi-GPU developer nodes.

### 4. Hot Chips 2026: Samsung and SK hynix Unveil Next-Gen AI Memory Architectures (Category 6)

**Source:** Hot Chips 2026 Technical Proceedings / Tom's Hardware / ServeTheHome / BusinessKorea / TrendForce  
**Event Date:** 2026-08-24/25 | **Publication Date:** 2026-08-26 (Technical session proceedings finalized)  
**Status:** Conference Technical Proceedings & Semiconductor Architecture Reveal  
**Primary URL:** https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/hot-chips-2026-samsung-lpddr5x-pim-and-sk-hynix-hbm4  

#### [Overview]
Why this research matters: In high-concurrency large language model serving, memory bandwidth and interface energy consumption (the physical power required to transfer bits between memory chips and compute logic) represent the foundational "memory wall". Transferring data over 2.5D silicon interposers consumes between 4 and 7 picojoules per bit (pJ/bit), while high thermal density in multi-die packages can destabilize DRAM cells if temperatures exceed 85°C to 95°C.

During technical sessions at the Hot Chips 2026 Symposium at Stanford University, South Korean memory leaders **Samsung Electronics** and **SK hynix** detailed their advanced semiconductor blueprints to overcome memory bandwidth and thermal constraints:
1. **Samsung LPDDR5X-PIM & Custom HBM:** Samsung unveiled detailed specifications for **LPDDR5X-PIM (Processing-in-Memory)**, embedding dedicated multiply-accumulate (MAC) arithmetic units directly into every internal DRAM bank. This eliminates inter-chip data movement, delivering a **3.01x inference speedup and an 8x effective bandwidth boost** for on-device and edge inference. For datacenter accelerators, Samsung detailed **cHBM4 (Custom HBM4)** utilizing an advanced logic base die equipped with a **Heat Path Block (HPB)** that covers 50% of the physical interface (PHY) area, reducing localized peak thermal hotspots by over **35%**. Looking further ahead, Samsung outlined **zHBM**, which vertically stacks DRAM directly atop the host processor die to achieve a claimed **70% power-efficiency gain and 230% higher DRAM bandwidth**.
2. **SK hynix 16-Hi HBM4 & Intel EMIB-T Packaging:** SK hynix detailed its **HBM4 architecture**, expanding the physical interface to **2,048 pins** (double that of HBM3e) and supporting up to **16-high DRAM die stacks (48GB per stack)**. SK hynix confirmed it will extend its advanced Mass Reflow Molded Underfill (MR-MUF) packaging through the HBM4 generation before transitioning to direct copper-to-copper hybrid bonding for HBM5, while collaborating with Intel Foundry to qualify HBM stacks over **Intel EMIB-T (Embedded Multi-die Interconnect Bridge)** advanced substrates alongside TSMC CoWoS lines.

Impact on physical board, package, and system designers: Integrating 2,048-pin HBM4 stacks and PIM logic fundamentally alters power integrity (PI: maintaining stable voltage supply during dynamic current steps) and signal integrity (SI: preserving high-frequency signal waveforms across PCB traces). Doubling the interface width requires substrate designers to manage ultra-dense micro-bump arrays (uBumps) and through-silicon vias (TSVs) to avoid parasitic capacitance. Furthermore, managing the localized thermal dissipation of logic base dies in cHBM4 requires system designers to optimize secondary liquid cooling loops to ensure the DRAM stacks remain below thermal refresh limits.

#### [Strategic Impact]

- **Technology & Ecosystem Perspective:** Establishes processing-in-memory (PIM) and vertical 3D logic-memory stacking as viable engineering paths to mitigate memory bandwidth bottlenecks in edge and datacenter inference.
- **Business & Market Dynamics:** Diversifies advanced packaging supply chains, enabling accelerator developers to leverage Intel EMIB-T packaging as an alternative to TSMC CoWoS capacity constraints.
- **Infrastructure & Hardware Implications:** Mitigates thermal density constraints in accelerator packages through embedded heat-path structures (HPB), enabling sustained high-frequency operation in liquid-cooled AI racks.
- **Investment Strategy:** Overweight advanced packaging toolmakers (hybrid bonding, wafer thinning), high-density substrate manufacturers, and specialized thermal interface material (TIM) suppliers.

#### [Technical Deep Dive]

- **Core Technical Specifications (SW/HW):** [Symposium Venue]: Hot Chips 2026 (Stanford Memorial Auditorium) | [Samsung LPDDR5X-PIM]: Integrated parallel MAC arithmetic units inside every memory bank; delivers 3.01x AI inference acceleration and 8x internal bandwidth | [Samsung cHBM4 & HPB]: Advanced logic base die with integrated Heat Path Block covering 50% of PHY area, reducing peak junction temperature by >35% | [Samsung zHBM Roadmap]: Direct vertical logic-on-memory 3D stacking claiming +70% power efficiency and +230% bandwidth | [SK hynix HBM4 Interface]: 2,048-bit Wide Bus Interface (doubled pin count vs. 1,024-pin HBM3e) with up to 16-Hi DRAM stacking (48GB capacity per stack) | [SK hynix Packaging Matrix]: MR-MUF packaging extended through HBM4; hybrid bonding targeted for HBM5; integration qualified across TSMC CoWoS (S/L/R) and Intel EMIB-T | **Source:** Hot Chips 2026 Technical Proceedings / Tom's Hardware / ServeTheHome (2026-08-25)
- **Comparative Analysis:** Traditional HBM3e utilizes a passive base die with 1,024 I/O channels operating over standard 2.5D interposers. Next-generation HBM4 doubles interface width to 2,048 pins and incorporates active logic functions into the base die, while PIM architectures move arithmetic operations directly into DRAM banks to cut interface energy consumption.

Without broad runtime framework support, developers may hesitate to rewrite inference kernels to utilize PIM arrays. Additionally, fabricating 16-Hi HBM4 stacks with 2,048 TSV connections introduces compound packaging yield risks during mass production.
