[CORE NEWS ALLOCATION]

The final report contains exactly 4 news items.

Default allocation: 3:1

- 3 items: AI industry / software / infrastructure / business / ecosystem
- 1 item: AI hardware / semiconductor / circuit / package / board / system engineering

This is the default because AI software, model, agent, business, and infrastructure events occur much more frequently than meaningful circuit-design research or board-level breakthroughs.

Dynamic 2:2 allocation

Use 2:2 instead of 3:1 when there are at least two genuinely high-impact hardware/circuit/board/system developments within the valid time window.

Do NOT force a second hardware item merely to achieve 2:2.

A hardware item qualifies for the second slot only when it represents a meaningful technological or architectural development, not merely:

- a routine product launch
- a minor benchmark improvement
- a marketing announcement
- a generic PCB tutorial
- a repeated report
- a low-impact academic paper
- a product teardown without broader architectural significance

Hardware priority rule

When choosing between a marginal AI software story and a genuinely important hardware/system development, prefer the hardware development.

However, never lower the evidence standard just to maintain a hardware quota.

[TECHNICAL WRITING RULES]

Write the report in English.

Use professional executive-level language.

The reader is technically capable but still developing expertise in advanced AI hardware.

For specialized terms, give a short plain-English definition at first use.

Examples:

PMIC — power-management IC that generates and regulates supply rails.

PDN — power-delivery network that distributes stable power from the regulator to the load.

SI — signal integrity, the ability to transmit high-speed signals without unacceptable distortion.

PI — power integrity, the ability of the power network to maintain stable voltage under dynamic load.

SerDes — serializer/deserializer used for high-speed serial data transmission.

Do not oversimplify quantitative specifications.

Retain exact figures from primary sources.
